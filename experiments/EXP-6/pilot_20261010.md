# EXP-6 memory-arm pilots, 10-10-2026

Two runs of the low-memory arm alone (`experiments/_logs/pilot_exp06_oom.py`, not committed:
`_logs` is ignored), cleaning and feature jobs, to find a heap at which an out-of-memory lands.
They measured only that - whether the fault lands - and not recovery. Raw lines as printed:

20 000 customers, at 460m:

```
PILOT {"first_error_class": null, "feature_first_error_class": null, "recovered": true, "features_succeeded": true, "attempts": 1, "feature_attempts": 1, "job_duration_s": 62.7, "feature_job_duration_s": 159.7, "feature_disk_spill_bytes": 190768941, "memory": "460m"}
```

No out-of-memory landed. 460m is about the least heap Spark starts with: it refuses a heap
below 1.5 times its 300 MB reservation, which the playbook classifies as
`RESOURCE_CONFIG_INVALID` and leaves to a person (DEC-20). So EXP-6's memory arm runs at 460m on
20 000 customers, where memory pressure is real (spill), and reports the out-of-memory claim as
untested unless the fault lands in the run itself.
